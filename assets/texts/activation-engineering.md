# Activation Engineering


There are many ways to perform steering of a LLM to a desired direction. It gets hard to know what tool to use for what kind of outcome. Lets make things clearer.

---

_**Methods:**_
- Additional steering
- Projectional suppression
- K-steering
- Self orgenising maps
- Direct optimisation
- Task vectors
- Patching

_**Additional directions**_
- Behavior / Topic / Bias
- Modality: Text, image, sound, robotic
- Goal: Suppression, detection, emphasizing
- Measures of the Quality loss
